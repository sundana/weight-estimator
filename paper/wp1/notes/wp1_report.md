## H1.2 — Taylor decomposition (tabular ground truth)

## H1.1 — deep policy-gradient alignment

`g_true` is the exact rollout policy gradient through the **real MuJoCo engine**: the
`InvertedDoublePendulum-v4` model is switched to the Euler integrator and each
integrator step is differentiated with `mujoco.mjd_transitionFD` inside a PyTorch
`autograd.Function` (`src/distractor_gym/deep/mujoco_diff.py`); `g_model` substitutes
the fitted ensemble. The policy input is the physics state `(qpos, qvel)` plus the
`d_d` analytic distractors (the native 11-d observation is not used). The diagnostic
rollout horizon is 8; the data-collection Gym horizon is 100. Six loss families are
compared: MLE, VAML-1, VaGraM, TD-error, calibrated, Lambert. Runs:
`runs/exp1_deep_alignment/alignment.json` (720 rows), plus calibration arms
`alignment_horizon.json`, `alignment_clip.json`, `alignment_control.json`,
`alignment_ceiling.json`. Tables: `h11_alignment.tex`, `h11_paired.tex`,
`h11_quality.tex`, `h11_horizon.tex`, `h11_clip.tex`, `h11_control.tex`.

- **Trainer normalization bug (found and fixed).** `fit_dynamics` trained the ensemble
  on **raw** `(s, a)` inputs while every inference path (`predict_mean`, `predict_torch`,
  `_model_mean`) standardized them, and the target loss was computed in raw next-state
  space (shrinking the effective learning rate by `sigma_y^2`). The model was therefore
  underfit at one step (scale-free error `0.4-0.8 sigma_y^2`), the `d_d = 0` reference
  gradient was not recovered, and family differences were buried in seed noise. After
  fixing `src/distractor_gym/deep/trainer.py` (standardize the model input; compute the
  loss in normalized target space) the sweep was regenerated; every number below is the
  corrected 10-seed result.
- **The null is recovered and the diagnostic is calibrated.** At `d_d = 0`, MLE is
  `+0.92 +/- 0.10` on medium and `+0.74 +/- 0.37` on random; at horizon 2 (a single model
  step) it is `+0.95`/`+0.99` (`h11_horizon.tex`), so the reference gradient is recovered
  and one-step underfit no longer limits the metric. One-step error is now `0.00-0.01` at
  `d_d = 0` (`h11_quality.tex`).
- **Mismatch appears at high `d_d`, and value-aware weighting does not robustly repair
  it.** Medium MLE falls `+0.92 -> +0.79` from `d_d = 0` to `50`, random `+0.74 -> +0.55`.
  In the paired table (`h11_paired.tex`, `Delta = cos(family) - cos(MLE)` within a seed)
  the only positive cells are calibrated on random high `d_d` (`+0.14`/`+0.15`,
  `t ~ 0.9`) and VAML-1 on random `d_d = 10` (`+0.17`, `t = 1.5`), both inside seed
  noise; medium `d_d = 50` is negative for every family (`-0.17` to `-0.36`). Lambert is
  the worst family on random data (`-0.30` to `-0.50`), and VaGraM is `~0` in every cell.
  The "value-weighted <=10% degradation" half of H1.1 is not supported by the gradient
  probe.
- **Random high-`d_d` is partial-return-limited.** `h11_quality.tex` shows the median
  gradient-norm ratio on random `d_d >= 10` is `0.01` with 75-80% of rows at
  `||g_model|| < 0.1` (locally flat model return), so the cosine is weak there despite a
  small one-step error.
- **Horizon sweep (`h11_horizon.tex`).** Medium `d_d = 0` is flat (`+0.95 -> +0.92` over
  horizons 2-8); random `d_d = 0` degrades with horizon (`+0.99 -> +0.74`) from
  compounding. At `d_d = 50`, calibrated beats MLE on random
  (`+0.90/+0.66/+0.67` vs `+0.87/+0.43/+0.38`).
- **Clip is not the instability source.** `h11_clip.tex` is flat for clip `2/3/6 sigma`
  (only random MLE moves, at `6 sigma`): the model rollouts stay inside the training
  band, so clipping never binds.
- **Identical-loss control.** At `d_d = 0` every family re-fit with the MLE objective
  yields exactly equal cosines (deterministic pipeline); the ceiling arm (model replaced
  by the true dynamics) gives `cos == 1` (`h11_control.tex`).
- **Specification fixes.** VaGraM now weights by `||grad_s V(s')||` at the *next* state;
  TD-error now uses `|V(s') - V(s_hat')|` (it previously compared `V(s)` against
  `V(s_hat')`); VAML-1 and Lambert were added to the deep family; `ReplayBuffer` stores
  `terminal` separately from episode-end so `fit_state_value` no longer treats
  time-limit truncation as terminal.
- **Determinism bug fixed.** `ReplayBuffer.sample` drew minibatches from the global
  `numpy` RNG, which `torch.manual_seed` does not reset, so the same seed produced
  different SAC replays depending on prior RNG history. It now takes a seeded
  `numpy.random.Generator` from `train_sac`; control and normal MLE match to 0.0
  (`tests/test_deep.py`).
- The transition Jacobian and the autograd `g_true` are validated against central
  finite differences (`tests/test_mujoco_diff.py`); a state-only reward makes
  horizon-1 policy gradients exactly zero, so the calibration sweep starts at
  horizon 2.
- **Verdict.** With the pipeline fixed, the deep gradient diagnostic still does **not**
  support H1.1: MLE degrades by `~15-25%` (not `>=40%`) from `d_d = 0` to `50`, no family
  beats MLE robustly, and the value-aware gains are within seed noise. The H1.1
  **policy-return** claim (>=40% MLE degradation) remains deferred to the full
  model-based training loop (WP2/WP3).

## H1.3 — per-sample VJP profiling

`experiments/exp1_profiling.py` sweeps batch size and VJP scheme on the local RTX 5060 Ti
(16 GB; the plan's A100/4090 is not available). Table: `paper/wp1/tables/h13_overhead.tex`,
verdict `runs/exp1_profiling/h13.json`.

- Exact online `torch.func.vmap(grad)` VJP is **dispatch-bound** (~1 ms/call, roughly flat
  in batch), giving `5-10x` overhead over the forward MLE baseline -> H1.3 **FAIL** here.
- **Stale-weight caching** restores the target: `K=10` gives `~0.7-0.9x` and `K=50`
  `~0.1-0.2x` of the forward cost.
- Central finite differences cost `~140-220x` and are not viable.
- This matches the plan's risk mitigation (if overhead >3x, fall back to stale caching or
  a TD-error surrogate). Absolute numbers are not reproduced; the overhead ratio is.
