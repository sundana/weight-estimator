## H1.2 — Taylor decomposition (tabular ground truth)

`decompose_td_error` now reports `spearman_product`; the uncapacitated tabular suite
(`runs/exp2/part_a.json`, 12 regimes) gives `rho = 0.999` in every regime (threshold
0.8), with `R^2 = 0.98` dense and `0.76-0.81` sparse and the curvature residual rising
under sparse reward. Table: `paper/wp1/tables/h12_lemma.tex`.

## H1.1 — deep policy-gradient alignment

MuJoCo dynamics are not differentiable, so `g_true` uses the exact analytic rollout
gradient of the differentiable `AnalyticDistractorEnv` (`src/distractor_gym/deep/analytic.py`);
`g_model` substitutes the fitted ensemble. Datasets are random-policy and medium-replay
SAC (`runs/exp1_deep_alignment/alignment.json`, 96 rows). Table: `paper/wp1/tables/h11_alignment.tex`.

- Random data: alignment is high at `d_d = 0` (`cos ~ 0.79` for all families) and
  degrades with `d_d` (0.30-0.74 at `d_d = 50`).
- Medium-replay data: alignment is lower and mixed, sometimes negative; no family
  dominates across distractor counts.
- The H1.1 **policy-return** claim (>=40% MLE degradation) is deferred to the full
  model-based training loop (WP2/WP3); this stage reports the gradient-cosine diagnostic.

## H1.3 — per-sample VJP profiling

`experiments/exp1_profiling.py` sweeps batch size and VJP scheme on the local RTX 5060 Ti
(16 GB; the plan's A100/4090 is not available). Table: `paper/wp1/tables/h13_overhead.tex`,
verdict `runs/exp1_profiling/h13.json`.

- Exact online `torch.func.vmap(grad)` VJP is **dispatch-bound** (~1 ms/call, roughly flat
  in batch), giving `5-10x` overhead over the forward MLE baseline -> H1.3 **FAIL** here.
- **Stale-weight caching** restores the target: `K=10` gives `~0.7-0.9x` and `K=50`
  `~0.1-0.2x` of the forward cost.
- Central finite differences cost `~140-220x` and are not viable.
- This matches the plan's risk mitigation (if overhead >3x, fall back to stale caching or
  a TD-error surrogate). Absolute numbers are not reproduced; the overhead ratio is.
